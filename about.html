About & Privacy

Cyber Threat Feed is a free, independent page that brings the cyber security industry's news, attacks and vulnerabilities into one place.

What this is

One place to see what is happening across cyber security, for security professionals, developers, system administrators, IT teams and leadership. It gathers recent attacks and breaches, actively exploited vulnerabilities, critical CVEs, vendor and government advisories, threat research and regulatory news from public sources, and refreshes about every 30 minutes.

Content belongs to the publishers

  • This site does not host or republish articles. It shows each item's headline, a short excerpt, the source name and date, taken from the publisher's own public RSS feed or API.
  • Every headline links directly to the original article on the publisher's site. Please read, cite and credit the original source.
  • All trademarks, names and content remain the property of their respective owners. Listing a source does not imply its endorsement.
  • If you publish one of these sources and want it changed or removed, open an issue and it will be handled promptly.

Your privacy

  • No tracking: no cookies, analytics, ads, fingerprinting or third-party scripts.
  • No accounts or forms: the site collects no personal data. Search and filters run entirely in your browser and are never sent anywhere.
  • No referrer: when you open an article, the publisher is not told you came from here.
  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security and operations, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. This site has no access to those logs.
  • Once you follow a link, the publisher's own privacy policy applies.

Use with care

  • Items are collected automatically and may be incomplete, delayed or wrong. Always confirm details, severity and fixes with the vendor or official advisory before acting.
  • Ransomware victim entries are claims made by criminal groups and tracked by ransomware.live. They are unverified, and a listing does not mean the organisation was breached.
  • Nothing here is professional, legal or security advice.
